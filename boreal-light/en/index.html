What you do

Boreal Light is a renewable energy engineering company based in Berlin. It builds solar plants that purify and desalinate water, off-grid and without diesel.

«Committed to Sustainable and Social Business, Ensuring Access to Safe Drinking and Irrigation Water.»

Simplicity

Plug-and-play container plants, built to work outdoors.

Affordability

In your words: reducing the cost of access to drinking water by 90%.

Off-grid

Powered by the sun. Water arrives where the power line does not.

What you offer

A family of solutions so there is good water where the grid does not reach. Every fact, with its source.

PlanetCube · solar desalination

Solar-powered reverse osmosis, no batteries or inverter. From 1,000 to 40,000 L/h. Sea, brackish or contaminated water.

The four stages

  1. The sale

    From the first recommendation to the signed contract. Those who recommend introduce us to whoever has the water problem.

  2. The installation

    From the site form to the first litre. Order to Berlin, water permits, works with the local partner.

  3. The funding

    From the call for proposals to the grant received. Projects ready when the window opens.

  4. The life of the plant

    Water every day, maintenance and the story told. That is what brings the next site.

What we propose

To be your representation in Spain. As a family project: unhurried, invested in, and built to last.

  1. Speaking your language here

    A website and materials in Spanish and Catalan, with a site form for quote requests: water source, consumption, distances, and grid or solar.

  2. Finding the first site

    A real customer with a real water problem. A pilot people can visit.

  3. A local installation partner

    A water and solar engineering or installation firm, close to the site, for the works and the maintenance.

  4. The water school

    Training for technicians, installers and communities. It is what we do best.

  5. Care and tell

    Maintain each plant and tell what changes. So that each site brings the next one.

There are no prices or terms here. We will talk about that in person, once we know what you want.

Building the company in Spain together

We would like to help create the company that distributes Boreal Light in Spain. As a family project, with our own investment and built to last. And with you inside, if you want.

  1. Getting to know each other

    The first conversation: what you want and what we can bring.

  2. Reviewing each other

    A review both ways. We look at products and references. You look at our network and our plan.

  3. Giving it shape

    The company, the territory and each party's role. Decided together.

  4. The first pilot

    A real site, with an installation partner nearby.

  5. Growing as a network

    The water school, maintenance and the cases that bring the next site.
