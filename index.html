The methodology

What org charts cannot show

Value Network Analysis (VNA), developed by Verna Allee, maps your organization as a living ecosystem of exchanges. Beyond linear processes, it reveals the invisible transactions that actually determine efficiency and innovation.

The formal deliverables — contracts, software, revenue — are only half the story. The other half is what your org chart never captures: trust, knowledge, favors, strategic information. The flows that make things work or quietly break them.

We have applied this methodology across multiple engagements with IKEA and global leaders, unlocking value flows that no conventional process analysis had detected.

Tangibles ——

What gets counted

Contracts, software, money, formal deliverables. Visible in your management systems.

Intangibles - - -

What moves everything

Trust, knowledge, favors, reputation. The flows no ERP records — but that determine outcomes.

→ The difference between an organization that delivers and one that stalls is rarely in the formal processes. It is in the unmapped intangibles.

The process

Four steps to systemic transformation

We don't audit. We map, diagnose and co-design with your teams — so the knowledge stays inside your organization.

01

Role Definition

We shift the lens from job titles to active roles in the value flow — exposing the real communication structure, independent of the official org chart.

02

Transaction Mapping

We identify MUST deliverables (contractual: code, money, reports) and EXTRA deliverables (intangibles: trust, favors, knowledge) that build durable relationships.

03

Network Diagnostics

We visualize where the network stalls — operationally or emotionally — detecting weak links and value flows that are not being reciprocated.

04

Systemic Redesign

Through high-impact insights, we resequence the network to create multiple optimized pathways — building agility and resilience into the ecosystem.

Ambition level

From Kaizen to Kaikaku

Kaizen · Continuous improvement

Optimize what exists

Most consultancies stop here. They identify inefficiencies in existing processes and propose incremental improvements. Necessary, but not sufficient.

Kaizen answers: How do we do better what we already do?

Kaikaku · Disruptive transformation

Reinvent the value flow

By mapping the complete network — tangibles and intangibles — we find the quantum leap: reconfiguring nodes and transactions to generate new business models or radical efficiencies.

Kaikaku answers: What entirely different value network can we build?

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Value Network Analysis?

A mapping method that analyzes an organization's value network systemically. Unlike linear process maps, VNA makes visible both tangible transactions (contracts, software) and the vital intangibles (trust, knowledge, empathy) that actually move teams.

Why is VNA necessary before implementing AI?

You cannot automate what you haven't mapped. Identifying exactly which value flow nodes can be handled by AI Agents — and which must remain human — requires the full network picture that VNA provides.

What types of organizations do you work with?

Primarily large organizations and clients who have experienced a Castellers workshop as a metaphor for a living organizational ecosystem. We also work with any institution or community seeking to improve their value flow and reach Kaikaku.

What is the difference between Kaizen and Kaikaku?

Kaizen seeks continuous, progressive improvement of existing processes. Kaikaku is a disruptive transformation — we completely rethink the value flow to generate new business models or radical efficiencies that were not visible from within.

What is SOS and how does it relate to VNA?

SOS (Swarm Operating System) is our internal tool built on VNA methodology. It demonstrates live how an AI agent ecosystem operates on a real value map — the practical proof of where VNA leads when combined with artificial intelligence.
